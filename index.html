A note from home

Dear Ayaan,

Ever since you left for college, it hasn’t felt any different than before. However, over the past two or three weeks, I’ve started to realize how much value you bring to this house.

Despite all the arguments with Mom and Dad that you never stopped until you were right, or the times you got mad at me for no reason, I still want you back in the house where you belong.

Enough about how I’m feeling. I hope you have fun on your birthday with your friends, whether you’re going to Halloween parties or just going to New York for a day or two.

In addition, I hope you keep having fun at Brown and can hopefully play line 1 on the club tennis team, like you did your senior year, or even become a student manager for the actual D1 team. Who knows?

Sincerely,
Azrain
